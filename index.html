Abstract

I'm a PhD student at Imperial College London, supervised by Dr. Matthew Wicker. My research is about formal verification in machine learning models, currently I am particularly working on using formal verification tools in connection with generalization bounds.

Keywords:Research interests:
  • Formal Verification
  • Generalization Bounds
  • Continual Learning
  • Model Robustness

1Introduction

While the empirical success of machine learning models and AI is very evident, the theoretical understanding for empirical patterns is often lacking. The main motivation behind my work is to mathematically understand and certify different machine learning model behaviours. Before my PhD, I studied Computer Science at The University of Manchester (BSc) and Imperial College London (MSc).

I'm always happy to talk about research and possible collaborations, so feel free to get in touch.
